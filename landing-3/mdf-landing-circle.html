Turning Today’s Challenges into Tomorrow’s Opportunities

Michel Daher Foundation (MDF) is a local Lebanese non-profit organization founded in 2014. Its mission is to improve the livelihoods of the Lebanese people, with a particular focus on the Bekaa community, thereby contributing to the Global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Our Support for Adira

Adira is a social enterprise supported by the Michel Daher Social Foundation, bringing together women from the Bekaa to prepare traditional Lebanese mouneh and catering. By creating opportunities to earn an income through their skills and culinary heritage, Adira helps women build more sustainable livelihoods while preserving the flavors and traditions of their communities.
